The Beanstalk app

One personal estimate of adult height, the range around it, and a growth log that stays on your phone.

Get the app

For teens and parents. iPhone and Android.

Coming soon to iPhone and Android

An estimate is not a promise. Nothing in the app or on this site makes anyone taller.

What you get

  • A personal estimate. Height, weight and both parents’ heights go into the Khamis-Roche method. You get one estimate and a 90% range.
  • An honest range. The range gets wider during puberty and when parents’ heights are from memory.
  • A growth log. Add measurements over time. See the growth rate and the percentile curve.
  • Signs to raise with a doctor. The app points out the screening signs pediatricians use. It does not diagnose.
  • Privacy. No accounts. Records stay on the device.

What you do not get

  • A promise. An estimate can be wrong, and the range says by how much.
  • A growth plan. Nothing in the app adds height.
  • Medical advice. For that, see a pediatrician.

Try the free tools first

The height predictor on this site shows the height percentile, a percentile projection and the mid-parental range. Read how the Khamis-Roche method works before you pay for anything.

Questions and answers

Is the Beanstalk app free?
The app will have free features. The full personal prediction is a paid result. Prices will be shown in the App Store and Google Play.
What do I need to enter?
Sex, age, height and weight, plus both parents’ heights. Measured heights give a better result than remembered ones.
How accurate is the app?
It uses the Khamis-Roche method. The published 90% error is about 5.3 cm for boys and 4.3 cm for girls. The app always shows the range, and widens it during puberty.
Will the app make me taller?
No. The app estimates and tracks. It cannot make you grow taller, and neither can anything else.
When is it out?
It is not released yet. It is coming to iPhone and Android.
