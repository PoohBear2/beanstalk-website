What Beanstalk does

Beanstalk is an app for teens and parents. You enter a height, a weight and both parents’ heights. The app gives one estimate of adult height and a 90% range around it.

The app also keeps a growth log. It shows the growth rate over time and flags the signs that doctors check. All of it stays on the phone. There are no accounts.

This website holds the free tools. They use the same growth-chart maths as the app.

What we will not tell you

  • We will not give one exact number. Every estimate here comes with a range.
  • We will not sell a way to grow. No food, pill, stretch or app can make you grow taller than your body was going to.
  • We will not hide the limits. Each method page says who the method was built on and how far off it can be.

What helps, and what does not

Adult height is mostly set by genes. Good habits support normal growth and health. They do not add extra height.

  • Sleep. Teens need 8 to 10 hours a night. Children 6 to 12 need 9 to 12 hours.
  • Movement. The World Health Organization advises an average of 60 minutes a day of activity for ages 5 to 17.
  • Food. Regular, varied meals with enough energy and protein. No supplement is needed for height.
  • Posture. Standing straight shows the height you already have. It does not change your bones.

Hanging and stretching do not make bones longer. They can add a few millimetres for less than an hour, because the spine decompresses. Then it goes back. Pills and powders sold for height do not work.

Questions and answers

Can anything predict adult height exactly?
No. Every method gives a range. The best method that needs no X-ray is right to within about 5 cm for 9 in 10 children. A bone-age X-ray read by a doctor is more accurate.
What is free on this site?
Today’s height percentile on the CDC chart, a percentile projection with a wide range, and the mid-parental height range. All of them run in your browser.
What does the Beanstalk app add?
One personal estimate from height, weight and both parents’ heights, using the Khamis-Roche method, with a 90% range. It also keeps a growth log on the device.
Will Beanstalk make me taller?
No. The app and this site cannot make you grow taller. Sleep, food and activity support normal growth and health. They do not add height.
