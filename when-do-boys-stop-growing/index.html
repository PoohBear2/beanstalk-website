When do boys stop growing?

Most boys are close to their adult height by 17 or 18. Late developers keep going for longer.

What the growth chart shows

The table shows the middle height for boys on the CDC chart. The last column shows how much that middle height rose in the year before.

The fastest year on the chart is 13 to 14, at about 7.8 cm (3.1 in). After 17, the middle height rises by less than 1 cm a year.

The chart is an average of many boys. One boy’s own spurt is sharper than the average and can come earlier or later.

Timing matters more than age

Puberty in boys usually starts between 9 and 14. A boy who starts early finishes early. A boy who starts late can still be growing at 18 or 19.

So “when do boys stop growing” depends on when puberty began. Late developers are short for their age for a while and then catch up.

Signs that growth is ending

  • The voice has broken. This is a late-puberty sign. On average it happens at 13.6 to 14.2 years.
  • Height has changed by less than 1 cm over a full year.

After 18

In one long study, men gained 2.1 cm (0.8 in) on average between 18 and 27. Most of that was in late developers. Many men gained nothing.

What helps, and what does not

Adult height is mostly set by genes. Good habits support normal growth and health. They do not add extra height.

  • Sleep. Teens need 8 to 10 hours a night. Children 6 to 12 need 9 to 12 hours.
  • Movement. The World Health Organization advises an average of 60 minutes a day of activity for ages 5 to 17.
  • Food. Regular, varied meals with enough energy and protein. No supplement is needed for height.
  • Posture. Standing straight shows the height you already have. It does not change your bones.

Hanging and stretching do not make bones longer. They can add a few millimetres for less than an hour, because the spine decompresses. Then it goes back. Pills and powders sold for height do not work.

When to see a pediatrician

This site is not medical advice. These are screening signs doctors use. Any one of them is a good reason to book a visit and bring your height records.

  • Height is below the 3rd percentile for age (about 2 standard deviations below average).
  • Growth is slower than 5 cm (2 in) a year between age 5 and the start of puberty.
  • The projected adult height is more than 10 cm (4 in) below the mid-parental target.
  • No signs of puberty by 13 in girls or 14 in boys.
  • Signs of puberty before 8 in girls or before 9 in boys.

Most children with one of these signs are healthy. A doctor can check, and a hand X-ray for bone age tells more than any calculator.

Questions and answers

At what age do boys stop growing?
Most boys are close to adult height by 17 or 18. On the CDC chart the median boy gains less than 1 cm a year after 17. Late developers can keep growing into their early twenties.
Do boys grow after 16?
Usually yes, a little. The CDC median for boys is 173.5 cm (5 ft 8.3 in) at 16 and 176.8 cm (5 ft 9.6 in) at 20. That is 3.3 cm (1.3 in) more. A late developer can gain much more than that.
Do boys grow after 18?
Some do. In one long study, men gained 2.1 cm on average between 18 and 27. Most of that gain was in men who matured late.
When is the growth spurt in boys?
On the CDC chart the median boy grows fastest between 13 and 14. One boy’s own spurt can come a couple of years earlier or later.
Does the voice breaking mean growth is over?
No. The voice breaks late in puberty, at 13.6 to 14.2 years on average. The fastest growth is mostly over by then, but slower growth goes on.
