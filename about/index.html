About Beanstalk

Height tools for teens and parents that say what is known, what is a guess, and what nobody can know.

What Beanstalk is

Beanstalk is a height-prediction app for iPhone and Android, plus this website of free tools. Both use the same calculation code.

The app gives one personal estimate with a 90% range. The website gives the height percentile, a percentile projection and the mid-parental range for free.

Our rules

  • Ranges, not single numbers. An estimate without its error is misleading.
  • Real sources. Every method is from published research. We list each one. See the methodology.
  • No growth promises. We do not sell a way to get taller, because there is none. Sleep, food and activity support normal growth and health. That is all we say about them.
  • No made-up trust signals. No invented reviews, ratings, experts or download counts.
  • Your data stays with you. See privacy.

What Beanstalk is not

It is not medical advice and not a diagnosis. A calculator cannot see a child. If growth worries you, see a pediatrician.

Questions and answers

Who makes Beanstalk?
Beanstalk is a small independent project. We are not doctors, and no doctor has reviewed this site yet. If that changes, the reviewer will be named on each page.
How does Beanstalk make money?
The website tools are free. The app will charge for the full personal prediction. There are no ads and we do not sell data.
Can I trust the numbers?
Check them. Every method and number is listed on the methodology page with its source, and we say which ones are not yet verified.

What the Beanstalk app adds

The app gives one personal estimate with a 90% range. It uses height, weight and both parents’ heights together. It also keeps a growth log on your device.

Coming soon to iPhone and Android

An estimate is not a promise. Nothing in the app or on this site makes anyone taller.
