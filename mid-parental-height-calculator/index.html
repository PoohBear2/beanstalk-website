Mid-parental height calculator

Parents' heights give a first, rough idea of a child's adult height. Here is the formula, the range and its limits.

The formula

Boy: (mother + father + 13 cm) ÷ 2

Girl: (mother + father − 13 cm) ÷ 2

Range: 8.5 cm above and below the target.

In inches: add or take off 5 inches, halve, and allow about 3.3 inches either side.

This is the Tanner method. Doctors call the result the target height.

A worked example

The mother is 165 cm. The father is 178 cm.

  • A son: (165 + 178 + 13) ÷ 2 = 178.0 cm (5 ft 10.1 in). Range: 169.5 cm (5 ft 6.7 in) to 186.5 cm (6 ft 1.4 in).
  • A daughter: (165 + 178 − 13) ÷ 2 = 165.0 cm (5 ft 5 in). Range: 156.5 cm (5 ft 1.6 in) to 173.5 cm (5 ft 8.3 in).

The error band

The band of 8.5 cm either side runs from about the 3rd to the 97th percentile for children of those parents. That is a spread of 17 cm, or almost 7 inches. So the formula narrows things down, but not by much.

Limits

  • It ignores the child. A child’s own height and growth so far say more than the parents do.
  • It works less well when the parents’ heights are very different, or when both are very tall or very short. Children of very tall or very short parents tend to end up closer to average than the formula says.
  • It assumes both parents reached their full height. Illness or poor nutrition in a parent’s childhood breaks that.
  • Remembered heights are often a bit too tall.

The height predictor puts this range next to the child’s own percentile. The Beanstalk app combines parents, height and weight in one estimate.

When to see a pediatrician

This site is not medical advice. These are screening signs doctors use. Any one of them is a good reason to book a visit and bring your height records.

  • Height is below the 3rd percentile for age (about 2 standard deviations below average).
  • Growth is slower than 5 cm (2 in) a year between age 5 and the start of puberty.
  • The projected adult height is more than 10 cm (4 in) below the mid-parental target.
  • No signs of puberty by 13 in girls or 14 in boys.
  • Signs of puberty before 8 in girls or before 9 in boys.

Most children with one of these signs are healthy. A doctor can check, and a hand X-ray for bone age tells more than any calculator.

Questions and answers

What is mid-parental height?
It is the adult height that the parents’ heights point to. Add the two heights. Add 13 cm for a boy or take off 13 cm for a girl. Then halve the result.
How accurate is the mid-parental height formula?
Not very, for one child. The usual range is 8.5 cm above or below the target. Most children land inside that range, and a few land outside it. Brothers and sisters often differ by several centimetres.
Why add or take off 13 cm?
Adult men are about 13 cm taller than adult women on average. The formula shifts the mother’s height up for a son, or the father’s height down for a daughter, before averaging.
What if I only know one parent’s height?
The formula needs both. With one parent missing, use the height percentile and the percentile projection instead.
Should parents be measured?
Yes, if you can. People often remember being a little taller than they measure. Measure without shoes, against a wall.
