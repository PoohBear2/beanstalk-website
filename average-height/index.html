Questions and answers

What is the average height by age?
It is the 50th percentile on a growth chart: half of children that age are taller and half are shorter. This table uses the CDC 2000 chart for the United States.
Are boys or girls taller?
They are about the same height until puberty. Girls start their growth spurt earlier. On this chart the median girl is taller than the median boy at 11, 12, 13. Boys grow for longer and end up taller on average.
Is the average the height a child should be?
No. The average is the middle, not a goal. Anywhere from the 3rd to the 97th percentile is in the usual range.

What the Beanstalk app adds

The app gives one personal estimate with a 90% range. It uses height, weight and both parents’ heights together. It also keeps a growth log on your device.
