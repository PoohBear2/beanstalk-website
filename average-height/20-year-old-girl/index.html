Average height of a 20-year-old girl

The average 20-year-old girl is 163.3 cm tall (5 ft 4.3 in). That is the middle of the CDC growth chart at exactly 20 years.

Height percentiles for girls at 20 years (CDC 2000)
Percentilecmft and in
3rd 151.1 cm 4 ft 11.5 in
10th 155.0 cm 5 ft 1 in
25th 159.0 cm 5 ft 2.6 in
50th (average) 163.3 cm 5 ft 4.3 in
75th 167.7 cm 5 ft 6 in
90th 171.6 cm 5 ft 7.6 in
97th 175.5 cm 5 ft 9.1 in

Read it like this: a 20-year-old girl who is 155.0 cm is taller than 10 in 100 girls the same age. One who is 171.6 cm is taller than 90 in 100.

Growth around age 20

  • From 19 to 20, the CDC median rises 0.1 cm (0 in).

The CDC chart ends at 20. The median here, 163.3 cm (5 ft 4.3 in), is the chart’s average adult height for women in this US reference group.

Girls are essentially done growing about 4 years after their first period. Girls who started puberty late can still be growing at this age.

No exercise, food or supplement adds height once the growth plates have closed.

About these numbers

The table uses the CDC 2000 height-for-age chart. It was built from US children measured between 1963 and 1994. It shows height at exactly 20 years. A child who is 20 and a half will be a bit taller, so add months in the calculator.

Average does not mean ideal. Any height from the 3rd to the 97th percentile is in the usual range.

When to see a pediatrician

This site is not medical advice. These are screening signs doctors use. Any one of them is a good reason to book a visit and bring your height records.

  • Height is below the 3rd percentile for age (about 2 standard deviations below average).
  • Growth is slower than 5 cm (2 in) a year between age 5 and the start of puberty.
  • The projected adult height is more than 10 cm (4 in) below the mid-parental target.
  • No signs of puberty by 13 in girls or 14 in boys.
  • Signs of puberty before 8 in girls or before 9 in boys.

Most children with one of these signs are healthy. A doctor can check, and a hand X-ray for bone age tells more than any calculator.

Questions and answers

How tall is the average 20-year-old girl?
163.3 cm (5 ft 4.3 in) at exactly 20 years old. This is the 50th percentile of the CDC 2000 growth chart, which was built from US children.
What height is short or tall for a 20-year-old girl?
Below 151.1 cm (4 ft 11.5 in) is under the 3rd percentile. Above 175.5 cm (5 ft 9.1 in) is over the 97th percentile. Most healthy 20-year-olds are between the two. A height under the 3rd percentile is worth showing a pediatrician.
Do girls still grow at 20?
Mostly not. The CDC median rises only 0.1 cm (0 in) between 19 and 20. Growth is essentially done about 4 years after the first period.
