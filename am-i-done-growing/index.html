Am I done growing?

Growth slows down before it stops. These are the signs that the end is near, and the numbers behind them.

Signs for everyone

  • Your height has stopped changing. Measure every three to six months, at the same time of day. No change over a year means growth is finished or nearly finished.
  • Puberty is complete. Height growth ends at the end of puberty, when the growth plates in the long bones close.

People are a little taller in the morning than in the evening. So always measure at the same time of day.

Signs in girls

The first period is the most useful marker. The fastest growth comes before it. After it, growth slows down.

  • Girls grow about 6.6 cm (2.6 in) on average after their first period.
  • If the first period came before 11, the average is about 8 cm (3.2 in).
  • If it came after 13, the average is about 4.4 cm (1.7 in).
  • Girls are essentially done about 4 years after the first period.

These are averages from one 2025 study. Try the growth after first period calculator, or read when girls stop growing.

Signs in boys

  • Voice break. This is a late-puberty sign. On average it happens at 13.6 to 14.2 years. After it, the fastest growth is mostly over, but growth goes on.
  • After 18. In one long study, men gained 2.1 cm (0.8 in) on average between 18 and 27. Most of that gain was in late developers.

More detail: when boys stop growing.

What you can check today

The height percentile calculator shows where you are on the growth chart. The height predictor shows the adult range that percentile points to.

Can I do anything to keep growing?

Adult height is mostly set by genes. Good habits support normal growth and health. They do not add extra height.

  • Sleep. Teens need 8 to 10 hours a night. Children 6 to 12 need 9 to 12 hours.
  • Movement. The World Health Organization advises an average of 60 minutes a day of activity for ages 5 to 17.
  • Food. Regular, varied meals with enough energy and protein. No supplement is needed for height.
  • Posture. Standing straight shows the height you already have. It does not change your bones.

Hanging and stretching do not make bones longer. They can add a few millimetres for less than an hour, because the spine decompresses. Then it goes back. Pills and powders sold for height do not work.

When to see a doctor

This site is not medical advice. These are screening signs doctors use. Any one of them is a good reason to book a visit and bring your height records.

  • Height is below the 3rd percentile for age (about 2 standard deviations below average).
  • Growth is slower than 5 cm (2 in) a year between age 5 and the start of puberty.
  • The projected adult height is more than 10 cm (4 in) below the mid-parental target.
  • No signs of puberty by 13 in girls or 14 in boys.
  • Signs of puberty before 8 in girls or before 9 in boys.

Most children with one of these signs are healthy. A doctor can check, and a hand X-ray for bone age tells more than any calculator.

Questions and answers

How do I know if I am done growing?
The clearest sign at home is no change in height over a full year, measured the same way. The only sure test is an X-ray that shows the growth plates have closed. A doctor decides if that is needed.
How much do girls grow after their first period?
About 6.6 cm on average. It is about 8 cm if the first period came before 11, and about 4.4 cm if it came after 13. Growth is essentially done about 4 years after the first period.
Do boys grow after their voice breaks?
Yes, usually. The voice breaks late in puberty, at 13.6 to 14.2 years on average. The fastest growth is mostly over by then, but growth usually goes on at a slower rate.
Can you grow after 18?
A little, sometimes. In one long study, men gained 2.1 cm on average between 18 and 27. Most of that was in men who matured late. Most women are done before 18.
Can I still grow after my growth plates close?
No. Once the growth plates have closed, you will not grow taller. Stretching, hanging and supplements do not reopen them.
