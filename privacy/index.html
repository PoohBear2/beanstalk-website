Privacy

Short version: we do not collect your data.

Last updated: 2026-11-12

This website

  • No cookies.
  • No trackers, analytics scripts or ad scripts.
  • No fonts or scripts loaded from other companies.
  • The calculators run in your browser. What you type is not sent anywhere.
  • Your unit choice (cm or ft/in) is saved in your browser’s local storage. Nothing else is saved.

The company that hosts the site handles each page request, as every web host does. That involves your IP address. We do not add any tracking of our own.

The app

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • Heights, weights, dates and other records are stored on the device.
  • If you delete the app, the records on that device are deleted with it.

Purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play under their own privacy terms.

Children

The site does not ask for names, email addresses or other personal details from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.

Contact

A contact address will be listed here before the app is released.

Questions and answers

Does this website use cookies?
No. The site sets no cookies and loads no trackers, ad scripts or analytics scripts.
Where do the numbers I type go?
Nowhere. The calculators run in your browser. Heights and ages are not sent to us or to anyone else.
What is saved in my browser?
One thing: your choice of centimetres or feet and inches. It is saved in your browser’s local storage so the site remembers it. You can clear it in your browser settings.
Does the app need an account?
No. The app has no accounts. Growth records are stored on the device.
