Growth after the first period

Most of a girl's growth comes before her first period. Some comes after. Here is how much, on average.

Before 118.2 cm (3.2 in)

After 134.4 cm (1.7 in)

About 3.8 cm (1.5 in) of this comes in the first year. Girls are essentially done about 4 years after the first period.

How the calculator works

It picks the average for your age at first period: before 11, between 11 and 13, or after 13. Then it takes off the share of growth that has usually happened already.

The first-year share comes from the study. After the first year, we spread the rest evenly up to 4 years. That even spread is our own assumption. The study does not give year-by-year figures that we could use.

The “most girls” figure is the top of a 90% range, based on the spread in the study.

Limits

  • These are averages from one study. Any one girl can grow more or less.
  • You need the age at first period to within a few months.
  • It does not use parents’ heights or the growth rate so far.

When to see a pediatrician

This site is not medical advice. These are screening signs doctors use. Any one of them is a good reason to book a visit and bring your height records.

  • Height is below the 3rd percentile for age (about 2 standard deviations below average).
  • Growth is slower than 5 cm (2 in) a year between age 5 and the start of puberty.
  • The projected adult height is more than 10 cm (4 in) below the mid-parental target.
  • No signs of puberty by 13 in girls or 14 in boys.
  • Signs of puberty before 8 in girls or before 9 in boys.

Most children with one of these signs are healthy. A doctor can check, and a hand X-ray for bone age tells more than any calculator.

Questions and answers

How much do girls grow after their first period?
About 6.6 cm (2.6 in) on average. Girls whose first period came before 11 grow about 8 cm more. Girls whose first period came after 13 grow about 4.4 cm more.
How long do girls grow after their first period?
Growth slows quickly. About 3.8 cm comes in the first year. Girls are essentially done about 4 years after the first period.
Why do girls with an early first period grow more afterwards?
They reach their first period at a younger age and a shorter height, with more growing time left. It does not mean they end up taller as adults.
Is 2 inches of growth after a first period normal?
Yes. The average is about 2.6 inches, and there is a wide spread around it. Some girls grow 1 inch more and some grow 4.
What if there is no first period yet?
Then this page does not apply yet. No signs of puberty by 13 is a reason to see a pediatrician.

What the Beanstalk app adds

The app gives one personal estimate with a 90% range. It uses height, weight and both parents’ heights together. It also keeps a growth log on your device.
